Status: exploratory development after the frozen experiment 26 transfer
failure. No protected target or checkpoint metric may be read.

## Question

Can reduced nuisance shrinkage control false support on the exact pooled PGDL
Tasks 6-9 metadata geometry without destroying the strong effect-0.50 power
observed in experiment 26?

## Fixed Development Screen

- 240 independent model rows with the exact task sizes and allowed metadata
  adapter from experiment 26.
- B1, B2, and B3 baseline ladders.
- Five known-null families, two reliability levels, and interaction increments
  at effects 0.20, 0.35, and 0.50.
- Twenty paired repetitions per cell under a new seed namespace.
- Configuration-level cross-fitted conditional-rank covariance, five folds,
  degree 2/4/6 interaction ridge candidates, 999 wild draws, and `p <= 0.001`.
- Ridge values are fixed in the runner before development outputs are inspected.

Candidates are screened by worst-cell raw null support, worst effect-0.50
positive power, estimability, and simplicity. Development cannot authorize a
protected analysis. A candidate must be frozen and pass a new 100-repetition
confirmation on disjoint seeds.

Every candidate and unfavorable cell is retained. Experiment 26 remains the
binding historical result for the prior rule.
